The U of I is already creating or providing access to a variety of AI tools.

## AI tools created by the U of I

- [MindRouter]({{ '/datahub/mindrouter.html' | relative_url }}) - The University's own AI service provider, managed by Research Computing and Data Services, and open to any Vandal account. Provides a no-code chat interface, as well as an API for developers.
- [Vandalizer](https://ai4ra.uidaho.edu/vandalizer/) - Allows users to pull structured information out of grant and contract documents, store it in a knowledge base, and use it to more quickly and efficiently complete research administration work.
- [UniVERSO](https://universo.uidaho.edu/) - A research-discovery tool built by the Library and the Institute for Interdisciplinary Data Sciences. Describe a topic in ordinary language and it returns U of I researchers whose work matches, drawn from profiles in the VERSO research repository.

## Other AI integrations

- [Microsoft Copilot](https://support.uidaho.edu/TDClient/40/Portal/KB/Article/3610/How-to-access-Microsoft-Copilot) - Microsoft's AI assistant, included in the University's Microsoft licensing, so staff, faculty, and students already have it. The knowledge base article walks through signing in with your U of I account and confirming you are in the University's protected environment rather than the consumer version.
- [Zoom AI Companion](https://support.uidaho.edu/TDClient/40/Portal/KB/Article/3024/Zoom-AI-Companion) - Meeting summaries and smart recording inside U of I Zoom accounts. It is off by default and the meeting host controls whether it runs.

## Building custom AI tools for your work

Anyone interested in using AI to help with their work can request assistance incorporating existing tools or developing a new one. The examples above are specialized tools, but also exemplify how AI can be used to:

- Extract and summarize information from batches of documents.
- Allow searching through information using natural language, rather than keywords.
- Automate repetitive data processing tasks.
- Triage and route incoming information or work.

For a list of AI contacts at the U of I, see the [U of I AI Guidance]({{ '/datahub/ai-guidance.html' | relative_url }}) page.

## Before you try one

Whether a given tool may hold a given piece of University information is settled
by the University's policy. See
[U of I AI Guidance]({{ '/datahub/ai-guidance.html' | relative_url }}) for which
services are approved for which kinds of data. To talk through applying any of
this to your own work, visit the [Data Hub]({{ '/datahub/' | relative_url }}).
