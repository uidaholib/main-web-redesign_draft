[MindRouter](https://mindrouter.uidaho.edu/) is the University of Idaho's own AI service. It runs on University
hardware. Anyone with a Vandal account can sign in, and using it needs no coding.

## What it is

MindRouter is a tool created by Research Computing and Data Services that allows the U of I community to use generative AI without sending their data to non-campus entities.

It provides a no-code-required chat interface, much like standard commercial AI tools. It also provides an API for anyone wanting to build generative AI applications.

It can search the web to respond to queries, and help process documents you
upload to it, but ultimately it still uses language models, with
the strengths and failure modes described on
[AI Basics]({{ '/datahub/ai-basics.html' | relative_url }}). That page is worth
reading first if this is your first AI tool.

## Why you might use it

For those new to AI, some first experiments could be:

- *Drafting and reworking text you will edit anyway*. A first pass at an announcement, a long document boiled down to its points, the same message rewritten for a different audience, or notes turned into prose.
- *Uploading a document and working from it*. Pull every deadline out of a funding
announcement, turn a form's requirements into a checklist a faculty member can
follow, or ask what changed between two drafts of a policy.
- *Asking about the software you use every day*. It will write an Excel formula,
explain one somebody else wrote, or help you troubleshoot a mail merge that will not
behave.

Because your text stays on University hardware, it's more likely you can work from the real message or the real document without violating privacy or confidentiality (see the *"What is safe to put in it"* section below).

Whatever you try, what you get back is a draft: verify any factual claims before using them.

## How to sign in

MindRouter is reachable only from the University network. On campus you are
already on it; from home, connect to the University VPN first.
[How to Install and Connect to University of Idaho VPN](https://support.uidaho.edu/TDClient/40/Portal/KB/Article/3816/How-to-Install-and-Connect-to-University-of-Idaho-VPN)
covers the setup.

1. Go to [MindRouter](https://mindrouter.uidaho.edu/).
2. Sign in with your Vandal credentials at the usual single sign-on page.
3. Hit the "Chat" tab in the top bar, type into the message box, and press Enter.

## What is safe to put in it

<div class="alert alert-info" markdown="1">
Which categories of University information may go into which AI service is set
by OIT. OIT's [Guidance for Artificial Intelligence](https://support.uidaho.edu/TDClient/40/Portal/KB/Article/3464/Guidance-for-Artificial-Intelligence){:.alert-link}
carries the table of approved services and the data risk levels each one is
approved for, MindRouter included. Check it before sending anything to MindRouter.
</div>

If you are unsure which category the information in front of you falls into,
[How to identify High Risk data](https://support.uidaho.edu/TDClient/40/Portal/KB/Article/1659/How-to-identify-High-Risk-data)
walks through it with concrete examples.
